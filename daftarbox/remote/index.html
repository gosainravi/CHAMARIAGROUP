Stay safe

✅ Only share with people you trust

When you share your screen, the other person can see everything on it — documents, passwords, personal files. Only connect with colleagues or IT support you know personally.

✅ You are always in control

This tool is view-only — the helper can see your screen but cannot move your mouse or press keys. Click Stop sharing or close the tab to end instantly.

✅ Codes expire each session

Your ID and one-time password change every time you open or refresh this page. Old codes cannot be reused.

⚠ We will never ask for your password

Chamaria Group IT staff will never ask for your Windows password, email password, or bank details via remote access.

⚠ No recording without consent

Sessions are peer-to-peer (direct device to device). No video is stored on any server. Only the two connected devices can see the stream.
