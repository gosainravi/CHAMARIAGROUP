Stay safe

✅ Only share with people you trust

When you share your screen, the other person can see everything. Only connect with colleagues or IT support you know personally.

✅ You are always in control

Click Stop sharing or close the tab at any time — the session ends instantly. The Control Agent also stops when you close its window.

✅ Codes expire each session

Your ID and one-time password change every time you open or refresh this page. Old codes cannot be reused.

⚠ We will never ask for your Windows password

Chamaria Group IT staff will never ask for your Windows, email, or bank password via remote access.

⚠ Peer-to-peer — nothing stored on servers

Video and control events go directly between the two devices. Nothing is recorded or stored.
