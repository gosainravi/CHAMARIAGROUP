About

Rajendra Chamaria

When business, life & family are aligned by purpose, wealth becomes a tool — not a goal.

Rajendra Chamaria is the founding patriarch of Chamaria Group — a visionary whose life's work embodies the belief that true wealth is born from the alignment of purpose, family, and enterprise.

His philosophy has shaped the group's culture from the ground up: building businesses that are not just profitable, but meaningful. Under his guidance, the group has grown into a diversified conglomerate spanning financial services, real estate, manufacturing, and packaging.

He believes that when a leader lives with purpose and treats people with dignity, the business becomes a force for genuine progress — for the family, employees, and the wider community.

Core Philosophy

Guiding Principles

01
Purpose Over Profit

Wealth is meaningful only when it serves a larger purpose. Every venture is grounded in the question: what value does this create beyond financial return?

02
Family as Foundation

Business and family are not competing forces — they are complementary. Aligned by shared values, they amplify each other's strength and resilience.

03
Integrity in Everything

Long-term trust is built on consistent integrity — with partners, employees, customers, and community. There are no shortcuts to a lasting legacy.
