What we check

The basics that stop most real-world attacks on small and mid-sized organisations — plus the Indian rules you are expected to follow.

  • Email spoofingSPF, DKIM and DMARC — can someone fake your email?
  • Domain safetyDNSSEC, CAA, MTA-STS and TLS reporting.
  • Accounts & MFALogins, admin rights and leavers.
  • Backups & ransomwareOffline copies and tested restores.
  • CERT-In rules6-hour incident reporting, 180-day logs, NIC/NPL time sync.
  • DPDP ActConsent, breach notice, deletion and children’s data.
  • Devices & patchingUpdates, antivirus, encryption and screen locks.
  • Network & Wi-FiFirewall, default passwords, guest Wi-Fi, remote access.
